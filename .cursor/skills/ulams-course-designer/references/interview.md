# Adaptive course interview

Ask only unresolved questions. Keep choices short, explain why each question matters for these
sources and always offer “decide for me”.

## Required decisions

1. **Audience and context** — role, reason for learning and where they will use it.
2. **Experience** — beginner/intermediate/advanced plus concrete prior knowledge.
3. **Outcomes** — what learners should be able to do, not only know.
4. **Duration** — total minutes and target lesson length.
5. **Experience shell**:
   - `course`: left outline, multi-page, resumable;
   - `scroll`: one continuous anchored page;
   - `slides`: self-paced one idea per screen.
6. **Activity balance** — balanced, practice-heavy, visual, scenario-led or minimal interaction.
7. **Assessment** — formative checks, final test, pass score, attempt limit and whether it is
   compliance/certification.
8. **Language and tone**.
9. **Delivery constraints** — phone/desktop, bandwidth/offline, assistive technology, embedded/LTI
   delivery and any deadline.
10. **Brand** — design-system/brand-guide source, logos, licensed fonts/assets; otherwise mood,
    light/dark, density, colour direction and imagery.

## Good defaults

- Shell: `course` over 30 minutes or with several modules; `scroll` up to about 20 minutes with a
  narrative; `slides` for concise visual linear material.
- Lesson length: 5–10 minutes unless the task requires an uninterrupted simulation or project.
- Activity balance: one meaningful retrieval/practice moment per objective cluster, not one widget
  per screen.
- Assessment: formative explanations by default; require pass/fail only when the learning purpose
  needs it.

## Additional questions when relevant

- Is the course facilitator-led or self-paced?
- Must completion issue a certificate or satisfy policy/audit requirements?
- Does an existing SCORM, cmi5, H5P, LiaScript or LTI activity need reuse?
- May learners upload projects, record audio or use camera/microphone?
- Is learner data allowed to leave the installation?
- Who owns and may reuse each logo, image, font and media asset?
