# EU VAT OSS for Digital Courses

**Note:** This document is not legal advice. Every rate or threshold should be verified against official EU sources.

| Option | How It Works | Seller Configuration | Risks |
|--------|--------------|----------------------|-------|
| Sylius Tax Zones | Configure tax zones within Sylius to apply VAT based on customer location. | Set up tax zones and rates in Sylius. | Misconfiguration could lead to incorrect tax calculations. |
| External Tax Service | Use a third-party service to handle VAT calculations. | Integrate the service with Sylius and configure it according to the service's instructions. | Dependency on a third-party service; potential for service downtime or data breaches. |
| Merchant-of-Record | Appoint a local merchant to handle VAT obligations. | Register with the local tax authority and ensure the merchant complies with local laws. | Additional administrative overhead; potential for disputes between the merchant and the seller. |
