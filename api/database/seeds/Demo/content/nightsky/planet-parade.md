# Planet parade 🪐

Our Solar System has **8 planets**. Four small rocky ones close to the Sun, and four giants far away. Let's meet them, one by one!

![Mercury]({{asset:planet-mercury.png}})

## Mercury — the speedy one
Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun. A year on Mercury lasts only **88 days**, so you would have a birthday every three months!

> 💡 **Fun fact:** Mercury has almost no air, so the day side is hotter than an oven (430 °C) and the night side is colder than Antarctica (−180 °C).

## Venus — the cloudy one
Venus is about the same size as Earth, but it is covered in thick yellow clouds. It is the **hottest planet**, even hotter than Mercury.

> 💡 **Fun fact:** Venus spins backwards! On Venus, the Sun rises in the west.

## Earth — our home 🌍
The only planet we know with liquid oceans on the surface and life. Light from the Sun takes **8 minutes** to reach us.

![Mars]({{asset:planet-mars.png}})

## Mars — the red planet
Mars is red because its dust contains **rust** (iron oxide). It has the tallest volcano in the Solar System, Olympus Mons, almost three times as tall as Mount Everest.

> 💡 **Fun fact:** Robots like Perseverance drive around Mars and send us photos. You will visit it in a 360° panorama in this mission!

![Jupiter]({{asset:planet-jupiter.png}})

## Jupiter — the giant
Jupiter is the **biggest planet**. More than 1,300 Earths would fit inside it. The Great Red Spot is a storm bigger than our whole planet, and it has been blowing for hundreds of years.

![Saturn]({{asset:planet-saturn.png}})

## Saturn — the one with rings
Saturn's rings are made of billions of pieces of ice and rock, from tiny grains to chunks as big as a house.

> 💡 **Fun fact:** Saturn is so light that it would float in a (very, very big) bathtub!

## Uranus — the sideways one
Uranus is tipped on its side, so it rolls around the Sun like a ball. It looks blue-green because of a gas called methane.

## Neptune — the windy one
Neptune is the **farthest planet** from the Sun. Its winds are the fastest in the Solar System: more than 2,000 km per hour.
