**Your adventure to the stars starts tonight!** Night Sky Explorers is a seven-mission journey for curious kids aged 10–14. With astrophysicist Dr. Ada Kowalczyk and Orbi the robot guide you will watch the Moon change, fly to Mars, see how stars are born and, at the end, build your very own star map.

## The missions

| Mission | What you do |
|---|---|
| 1. Lift-off | meet Orbi, find tonight's sky on a star chart |
| 2. Our Moon | move the Moon around Earth, start a 30-day Moon diary |
| 3. Planets | meet all eight planets, listen to Saturn, fly to Mars in 360° |
| 4. Stars | watch a star being born, flip through the life of a star |
| 5. Constellations | connect the stars in a game, go on a backyard sky safari |
| 6. Galaxies | find out how big space really is, take the mission quiz |
| 7. Your star map | draw or photograph your own constellation map |

Each mission takes **10–15 minutes**. Earn stars, keep a streak and unlock badges: Moon Watcher, Planet Hopper, Star Finder and Junior Astronomer.

## For parents

- **Safe and ad-free.** No chat with strangers, no ads, no in-app purchases for kids.
- **Progress emails.** A short weekly note on what your child explored, with an idea for a family night outside.
- **Offline too.** Most activities happen under the real sky, with a printable Moon diary and star map.
- Mission 1 is **free**. The whole course is included in the family subscription (€6/month).

## For teachers

The classroom package gives you up to 30 pupil accounts, printable worksheets and a teacher's guide for an astronomy club. Polish narration is available for every video.

Finish all seven missions to earn the **Junior Astronomer** badge and a printable diploma.
