# Build your star map ⭐

This is your final mission, explorer! You are going to make a map of the stars that **you** can see.

## Step 1 — Go outside
Pick a clear night and go outside with a grown-up. Let your eyes get used to the dark for 10 minutes. Bring a torch with red paper over it (red light keeps your night vision).

## Step 2 — Draw or photograph
Choose one:

- **Draw** the brightest stars on the star map worksheet (in the resources below). Join them with lines.
- **Photograph** the sky with a phone on a steady surface, then draw lines on a printout of the photo.

Try to include at least **one real constellation** (the Big Dipper, Cassiopeia or Orion) and **one constellation you invent yourself**.

## Step 3 — Name it and tell its story
Give your own constellation a name. What does it look like? Write 2–3 sentences about its story on the back of your map.

## Step 4 — Upload it
Take a photo or scan of your map and upload it here (JPG, PNG or PDF).

Dr. Ada will look at every map and send you a reply with a **sticker**. 🌟
