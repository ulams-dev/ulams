# Bonus: space jokes 😄

Orbi collected these on the way back from Mars. Tell them at dinner!

**Why did the Sun go to school?**
To get a little *brighter*. ☀️

**How do you throw a party in space?**
You *planet*! 🎉

**What kind of music do planets like?**
*Nep*-tunes! 🎵

**Why did the cow go to space?**
To see the *moooon*. 🐄🌙

**What do you call a robot who takes the long way around?**
R2-*Detour*. 🤖

**How does the Moon cut his hair?**
*Eclipse* it! ✂️

**What is an astronaut's favourite key on the keyboard?**
The *space* bar! ⌨️

**Why is Saturn so good at parties?**
Because it has lots of *rings* to invite people with. 💍
