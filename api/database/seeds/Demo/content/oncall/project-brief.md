# Postmortem assignment

Write the blameless postmortem for the game-day outage you commanded in module 3 (or for the outage simulator scenario, if you missed the drill).

## Requirements

- Use the template from the resources of this topic, in **Markdown or PDF**.
- **Timeline** in UTC, from the first trigger to the all-clear, at least 8 entries.
- **Impact** in numbers: duration, share of requests affected, error budget consumed.
- At least **three contributing factors**, none of which names a person.
- At least **four action items**, each with an owner, a due date and a type (*prevent*, *detect*, *mitigate*). At least one must be *detect* or *mitigate*.
- Maximum **1,200 words** plus the timeline.

## Review

Your postmortem is reviewed by **one peer** from your cohort and by **a tutor**. Expect comments within three working days. Status *Changes requested* is normal: most postmortems need one more round.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Excellent (3) | Adequate (2) | Needs work (1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Timeline | complete, precise, sourced from the scribe log | complete but vague times | gaps or reconstructed from memory |
| Impact | quantified, linked to the SLO | partly quantified | qualitative only |
| Contributing factors | several interacting conditions, systemic | a few, some point at individuals | single "root cause" or blame |
| Action items | specific, owned, dated, balanced types | specific but missing owners or dates | generic ("be more careful") |
| Tone | blameless and readable by the whole org | mostly neutral | defensive or accusatory |

Maximum score: 15 points. Passing: 10.
