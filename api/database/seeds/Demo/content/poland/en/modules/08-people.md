::: richtext title="Reading survey figures" duration="8 min"
**A survey asks people.** CBOS interviews a sample of adults. The share who say they are satisfied with their lives is a statement of opinion at a point in time, not a count of something. In the late-2025 survey 81% said they were satisfied with their lives, 86% with where they live and 70% with their material conditions; 55% looked at their own future with satisfaction, and financial satisfaction rose six points in a year {{src:PL-012}}.

**Activities.** In the 2025 CBOS survey 80% went to a restaurant with family or friends, 71% hosted friends at home, 67% gave money to charity and 64% read a book for pleasure {{src:PL-133}}. These are shares of adults who said they did it at least once in the year, not how often.

**A recorded amount.** Recorded alcohol consumption fell to 8.3 litres of pure alcohol per person in 2025, about 15% less than in 2019 {{src:PL-097}}. "Recorded" means what is in the statistics: it leaves out what is not.

**Correlation and cause.** Two lines moving together do not show that one caused the other. This course reports the figures and their sources. It does not claim to know why they changed.
:::
