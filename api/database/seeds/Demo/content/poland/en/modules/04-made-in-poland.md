::: richtext title="Reading company and mission figures" duration="10 min"
**Company-reported numbers.** Most figures in this chapter come from the companies' own results. That is a primary source for what the company says it did, not an independent audit. InPost ended 2025 with 61,196 parcel machines, more than half outside Poland, and handled about 1.4 billion parcels, up 25%; just over half its revenue now comes from abroad {{src:PL-063}}. Żabka runs 12,339 stores, including 173 in Romania {{src:PL-066}}. Solaris delivered 1,631 vehicles to 15 countries in 2025, 86% of them low- or zero-emission, and won an order for 107 trolleybuses for Vancouver {{src:PL-074}}. The Witcher games have sold 85 million copies and Cyberpunk 2077 35 million {{src:PL-077}}.

**A valuation is not money raised.** ElevenLabs was valued at $22 billion in an employee tender announced on 30 September 2026 {{src:PL-044}}, after ending 2025 with $330 million in annual recurring revenue {{src:PL-045}}. A tender lets employees sell existing shares; the company does not receive that money.

**People as well as firms.** OpenAI's chief scientist is Jakub Pachocki {{src:PL-047}}; Wojciech Zaremba was one of its founders {{src:PL-048}}. The University of Warsaw has reached the ICPC programming world finals 31 years in a row {{src:PL-129}}.

**Science in orbit.** In summer 2025 Sławosz Uznański-Wiśniewski flew to the International Space Station on the Ax-4 mission, Poland's first government-sponsored human spaceflight to the station. His 20-day mission, Ignis, carried 13 Polish-led experiments {{src:PL-131}}.

**Jobs.** Unemployment was 3.1% in 2025 on the EU's harmonised measure, and 78.8% of people aged 20 to 64 were in work {{src:PL-010}}. Headline unemployment is a figure for the whole population and says little about how easy it is to find a first job.
:::
