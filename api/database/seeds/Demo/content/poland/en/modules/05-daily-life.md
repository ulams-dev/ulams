::: richtext title="Reading the digital-state numbers" duration="8 min"
**Issued is not used.** mObywatel has issued 11 million mDowód ID cards and averaged 1.5 million logins a day in 2025 {{src:PL-019}}. Nearly 7.5 million people have reserved their PESEL number against identity fraud, three quarters of them through the app {{src:PL-024}}, and 20 million have activated an Internet Patient Account {{src:PL-029}}. Cards issued say how many people have the tool, logins say how often it is used, and neither says how satisfied people are.

**Counts and shares.** In the 2026 filing season, for 2025 income, almost 24 million income-tax returns were filed electronically against about one million on paper. The pre-filled Twój e-PIT service handled 14.8 million of them, and more than 35% were done in under five minutes {{src:PL-026}}. 24 million out of 25 million is about 96%: always say what the whole is before quoting a share.

**Payments.** BLIK processed about 2.9 billion transactions worth PLN 441.5 billion in 2025, across 20.7 million active accounts; 735 million were phone-number transfers between people {{src:PL-038}}. The average transaction was therefore about PLN 152, our calculation.

**An illustrative day.** The day on the map is built from services that exist, such as BLIK, the patient account, parcel lockers, mStłuczka and the qualified signature in mObywatel {{src:PL-033}}. No time savings are measured; it shows what is possible, not what is typical.
:::
