**Poland, read from the data.** This free course reads Poland on an interactive map and in charts, one chapter at a time. Every number names its public source, so you can open it and check.

## How the course works

- **Explore.** Each chapter opens a map or a chart you can move around and filter.
- **Read.** The text beside it explains what the picture shows. Numbered footnotes lead to the sources.
- **Check yourself.** Short quizzes tell you straight away what you got right.

## What you get

The course is free, open to everyone and comes with a certificate when you finish it. A Polish course, "Polska w liczbach", is planned as a separate course.

This page describes the course as it will be. The chapters are added step by step; for now the welcome lesson shows how a lesson is built.
