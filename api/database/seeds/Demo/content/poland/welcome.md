# Welcome to Poland, measured

Statistics are easier to trust when you can see where they come from. In this course every map layer and every chart names its source, and the text links to it.

## How a lesson works

1. **Explore the map or chart.** Move it, filter it, compare years.
2. **Read the explanation.** Numbered footnotes in the text lead to the public sources.
3. **Check yourself.** A short quiz closes the chapter.

Every step of a map or chart has a text version, so you can follow along without the graphics, with reduced motion, or with a screen reader.

## What comes next

The chapters are being added. When they arrive they appear in this course, and your progress is kept. You can open this lesson again at any time.
