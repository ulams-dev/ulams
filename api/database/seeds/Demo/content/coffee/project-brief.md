# Design your recipe

Your final project is a recipe that is yours: tested, written down and explained.

## The brief

1. **Choose one coffee** you like and know its origin, process and roast date.
2. **Brew three variations** of the same recipe and change **one variable** each time: grind, ratio, water temperature or pour schedule.
3. **Photograph** each variation: the brewer, the cup, and the spent coffee bed (it tells us a lot about your pouring).
4. **Taste and score** each cup out of 10 with at least three descriptors from the flavour wheel.
5. **Fill in the recipe card** (PDF in the resources of this topic) for your favourite variation and explain in 3–5 sentences why it won.

## What to submit

- the completed recipe card as **PDF or image** (JPG/PNG),
- the three photos (one file or a collage is fine).

## How we review it

| Criterion | What we look for | Points |
|---|---|---|
| Method | one variable changed at a time, numbers written down | 4 |
| Tasting | specific descriptors, honest scores | 3 |
| Reasoning | the choice follows from what you tasted | 2 |
| Presentation | readable card, clear photos | 1 |

Tomasz or Inés will reply within five working days with feedback and one suggestion for your next experiment. You may resubmit as many times as you like.
