**Open the Scottish Book.** This free course follows the mathematicians of the Lwów School through the problems they set each other, and lets you try the ideas yourself in live interactives before you read the explanation.

## How the course works

- **Play.** Each notebook starts with an interactive you can work with: a pattern to explore, an experiment to run.
- **Read.** A short story and explanation say what you found and where it came from. Historical statements are checked against published sources and cited.
- **Check yourself.** Short quizzes tell you straight away what you got right.

## What you get

The course is free, open to everyone and comes with a certificate when you finish it.

This page describes the course as it will be. The notebooks are added step by step, after their facts have been checked; for now the welcome lesson shows how a lesson is built.
