# Welcome to the Scottish Book

The Scottish Book was a notebook in which mathematicians wrote down problems for each other. This course takes some of those problems, and the people around them, and lets you meet each idea by doing it first.

## How a lesson works

1. **Play with the interactive.** Try the idea before it is explained.
2. **Read the story.** A short text says what you found and cites its sources.
3. **Check yourself.** A short quiz closes the notebook.

Every step of an interactive has a text version, so you can follow along without the graphics, with reduced motion, or with a screen reader.

## What comes next

The notebooks are being added, each one after its facts have been checked against sources. When they arrive they appear in this course, and your progress is kept. You can open this lesson again at any time.
