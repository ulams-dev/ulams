::: richtext title="5.1 The 1951 idea" duration="6 min" intro="A short, factual lesson. It gives dates and names, no technical detail."
In early 1951 Ulam proposed that strong compression of the thermonuclear fuel, driven by a fission bomb, was the key. Edward Teller proposed that radiation from the fission stage should do the compressing {{src:U-19,U-01}}. Their joint report, LAMS-1225, is dated 9 March 1951 {{src:U-19}}.

The first test of the design, the "Mike" shot of Operation Ivy, took place on 1 November 1952 at Enewetak Atoll. The history of the Defense Nuclear Agency calls it the "first thermonuclear" detonation {{src:U-32}}.

Who deserved credit was disputed between Teller and Ulam for decades {{src:U-19}}.
