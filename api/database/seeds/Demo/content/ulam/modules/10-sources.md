## How the facts were checked

Every person, date and number in the lessons was checked against the pages of the sources below, and the fact sheet that records each fact with its sources is in the repository (`demo-content/ulam/facts.json` and `docs/plans/interactive-demos-ulam-facts.md`). Where careful sources disagree, the lesson says so. A few statements came from secondary sources only and are marked in the fact sheet; none of them is used in a quiz. Short quotations are exact.
