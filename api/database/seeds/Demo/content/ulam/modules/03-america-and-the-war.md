::: richtext title="3.1 Princeton and Harvard" duration="7 min" intro="How Ulam came to the United States."
At the end of 1935 Ulam accepted von Neumann's invitation to the Institute for Advanced Study in Princeton. He arrived in New York in January 1936, then joined Harvard's Society of Fellows, and spent the summers of 1936 to 1939 in Lwów {{src:U-01,U-12}}.

For the academic year 1939 to 1940 he was a lecturer at Harvard. In the autumn of 1939 he met Françoise Aron at a party in Cambridge {{src:U-01}}.
:::

::: richtext title="3.2 August 1939" duration="7 min" intro="The last weeks in Poland, and a conversation about the book."
In August 1939, a few weeks before the war, Ulam sailed to New York with his 17-year-old brother Adam {{src:U-01}}. Before he left he talked with Mazur about the Scottish Book. Ulam's 1957 preface puts the conversation "a few days before I left Poland, around August 15", and Mazur said that if the city were bombed he would put the manuscripts and the book into a case and bury it {{src:U-12}}. It was a plan, and Ulam's account does not say whether it was carried out.

The dates of that August do not quite fit together: Ulam put the conversation around 15 August, while MacTutor's records have the ship leaving earlier. That is the subject of the box below.
:::

::: richtext title="3.3 Madison and the family's fate" duration="9 min" intro="Wisconsin, marriage, citizenship, and the call to Los Alamos."
Ulam taught at the University of Wisconsin in Madison from 1940 (some reference works give 1941) until 1943 {{src:U-01,U-04}}. He married Françoise Aron in Madison on 19 August 1941 and became a United States citizen in 1943 {{src:U-01,U-30}}.

In 1943 he was invited to Los Alamos at von Neumann's suggestion. He arrived there with Françoise on 4 February 1944, and their daughter Claire was born in Santa Fe on 15 July 1944 {{src:U-01,U-30}}.

His mother died in Vienna in 1938. MacTutor writes: "Ulam's father was also killed when the Germans occupied Lwów in 1941". According to the account of a relative that MacTutor quotes, his sister Stefania and her baby were also killed {{src:U-01}}. The course adds nothing to these words.
:::
