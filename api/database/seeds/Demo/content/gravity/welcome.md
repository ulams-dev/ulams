# Welcome to Gravity Lab

Gravity is the reason planets stay on their orbits, the Moon raises tides and a stone you let go of falls. In this course you will see those effects instead of just reading about them.

## How a lesson works

1. **Explore the simulation.** Move through the model and watch what changes.
2. **Read the explanation.** The text next to it names what you are seeing.
3. **Check yourself.** A short quiz closes the lesson.

Every step of a simulation has a text version, so you can follow along without 3D graphics, with reduced motion, or with a screen reader.

## What comes next

The interactive lessons are being added. When they arrive they appear in this course, and your progress is kept. You can open this lesson again at any time.
