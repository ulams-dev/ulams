**Learn gravity by flying through it.** This free course explains how gravity shapes the solar system, and you learn it inside a live, interactive model of the solar system: you move through the simulation yourself, read a short explanation beside it, and check what you understood with a quiz.

## What you will learn

Nine modules follow the walkthrough of the simulation:

1. **What gravity is**: one rule between any two masses, and how it built the Sun and the Earth.
2. **Falling around**: orbits as falling that keeps missing.
3. **Kepler's laws**: ellipses, equal areas and P² = a³.
4. **Cosmic velocities**: 7.9, 11.2 and 16.7 km/s.
5. **The Earth and the Moon**: tides, the barycentre and a lumpy Earth.
6. **Orbits in 3D and a moving Sun**: tilts, spin, precession and helices.
7. **The solar system as a machine**: light, the magnetic shield, spheres of influence, Lagrange points and resonance.
8. **Travelling by gravity, and beyond**: Voyager, the heliosphere, exoplanets, the Milky Way and dark matter.
9. **Einstein's gravity**: curved spacetime, Mercury, GPS, black holes and gravitational waves.

## How the course works

- **Explore.** Each lesson plays a part of the live simulation. Every step of it also has a text version and a still image, so the lesson works without 3D or with reduced motion.
- **Read.** A short explanation names what you are looking at and gives the numbers, each with its NASA, JPL or other source.
- **Practise.** Flip cards, a timeline, a table or a practice activity, and a quiz in each module. A final test of 15 questions closes the course.
