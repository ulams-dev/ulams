**Learn gravity by flying through it.** This free course explains how gravity shapes the solar system, and you learn it inside a live, interactive model: you move through the simulation yourself, read a short explanation beside it, and check what you understood with a quiz.

## How the course works

- **Explore.** Each lesson centres on an interactive simulation. Every step of it also has a text version and a still image, so the lesson works without 3D or with reduced motion.
- **Read.** A short explanation sits next to the simulation and says what you are looking at.
- **Check yourself.** Short quizzes tell you straight away what you got right.

## What you get

The course is free, open to everyone and comes with a certificate when you finish it.

This page describes the course as it will be. The lessons themselves are added step by step; for now the welcome lesson shows how a lesson is built.
