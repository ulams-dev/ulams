## How the facts were checked

Every number in the lessons was checked against the NASA, JPL or other reference named below. The orbital elements of the planets in the simulation follow the JPL tables of Standish and Williams. Where a figure in the simulation differs from the reference, the course uses the reference: the full list of checked claims is in the repository, in `demo-content/gravity/FACTCHECK.md`.
