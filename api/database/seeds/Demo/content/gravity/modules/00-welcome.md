::: richtext title="Welcome to Gravity Lab" preview=1 duration="5 min" intro="How the course works and what comes next."
Gravity is the reason planets stay on their orbits, the Moon raises tides and a stone you let go of falls. In this course you will see those effects in a live model of the solar system, then read the short explanation beside it and check yourself with a quiz.

## How a lesson works

1. **Explore the simulation.** The solar system stays behind the text. Use Back and Next, or the step list, to move through the lesson. Drag to rotate the view.
2. **Read the explanation.** A short text names what you are looking at and gives the numbers.
3. **Practise.** Flip cards, a table, a timeline or a practice activity, then a short quiz that tells you straight away what you got right.

Every step of the simulation has a text version, so you can follow along without 3D graphics, with reduced motion, or with a screen reader.

## The nine modules

1. What gravity is
2. Falling around: orbits
3. Kepler's laws
4. Cosmic velocities
5. The Earth and the Moon
6. Orbits in 3D and a moving Sun
7. The solar system as a machine
8. Travelling by gravity, and beyond
9. Einstein's gravity

A final test and a certificate close the course.

## Where the facts come from

Every number in the lessons comes from NASA, JPL or another named reference, and each lesson lists its sources. The orbital elements in the simulation follow the JPL tables {{src:G-13}}. Planet and moon properties follow the NASA planetary fact sheets {{src:G-04}}.
