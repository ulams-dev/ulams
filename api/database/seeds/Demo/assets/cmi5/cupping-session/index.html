Cupping at home

The Coffee Atlas · Chapter V · guided session, about 30 minutes
connecting…

You need: two coffees, a scale, a grinder, 4 cups or glasses, a spoon per coffee, water just off the boil (93–96 °C) and a cup of clean water to rinse. Use 8.25 g of coffee per 150 ml of water (the SCA cupping ratio), ground medium-coarse.

  1. Dry fragrance. Grind each coffee straight into its cup. Smell the grounds; note sweet, fruity, nutty or roasty notes.
    4:00
  2. Pour and steep. Fill each cup to the brim in one pour, start the timer and leave the crust alone for four minutes.
    4:00
  3. Break the crust. Push the crust back three times with the spoon, nose close to the cup: this is the strongest aroma you will get. Skim off the foam.
    1:00
  4. Taste as it cools. Slurp loudly from the spoon at about 70 °C, again at 60 °C and at room temperature. Sweetness and acidity open up as the cup cools.
    10:00
  5. Score your best cup. Use the form below. Score each attribute from 6 (good) to 10 (exceptional).
