# Tea Basics

A short guide to brewing loose-leaf tea at home.

## Leaves and water

### Green tea

Green tea is not oxidised and tastes grassy and fresh. Brew it with water at 70 to 80 °C for two to
three minutes. Boiling water makes green tea bitter.

### Black tea

Black tea is fully oxidised and tastes malty and strong. Brew it with water just off the boil, around
95 °C, for three to five minutes. A longer steep makes it stronger and more astringent.

## Steeping

### How much leaf

Use about 2 to 3 g of leaf per 250 ml of water. Large, open leaves need a little more by weight than
small, tightly rolled ones.

### Re-steeping

Good loose-leaf tea can be steeped several times. Each steep tastes different: the first is often
lighter, the second fuller. Add 30 seconds to the steeping time for each new steep.
