The **Lesson view** button at the top of a lesson (and on the course page) lets you choose how lessons look. Only you see these choices, they are kept per course, and they never change your results, progress or certificates.

## What you can choose

- **Text spacing**: comfortable, or compact with less space between blocks. Text size and contrast stay the same.
- **Lesson parts**: the whole lesson on one page, or a reading shown one part at a time. The parts follow the lesson's own headings; a reading with fewer than two headings stays whole. With the parts view, **Previous part** and **Next part** move between them, the position ("Part 2 of 4") is read out, and the lesson can be marked as read only once you reach the last part.
- **Navigation**: the course overview next to the lesson, or step by step, where the program stays closed behind its button and **Next** leads the footer.
- **Hints shown at the start of an exercise**: none, or the first 1 to 3 hints are already open. The rest still open one at a time.

Choose **Save lesson view** to apply it, or **Reset to suggested** to remove your choices. The form is a plain form: it works without JavaScript.

## The suggested lesson view

When your academy has adaptive presentation switched on and some lessons in a course have been hard for you, the lesson view may be adjusted for you, for example in parts with two hints open. A note says so, **Why was it adjusted?** lists the reasons in plain language, and **Show the whole lesson** switches it back. Your choice is remembered until you reset it.

## With a keyboard or a screen reader

The button opens a labelled form of radio groups that you reach with Tab and change with the arrow keys; Escape closes it and returns focus to the button. Nothing moves when your system asks for reduced motion.
