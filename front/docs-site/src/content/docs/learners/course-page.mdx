`/courses/<id>` shows one course of the tenant. It is public: anyone can open it without
signing in. An unknown course id shows the [404 page](/learners/error-pages/).

<Screenshot name="learner-courses-id" alt="A course page with the course header, the program and the enrol card" />

## What is on the page

- **Header.** Level and number of lessons, title, subtitle, summary, up to six facts (level,
  length, language and others from the course), the course image and up to four tutors. The
  buttons are "Start learning" (or "Continue learning") and, when the course has a free-preview
  topic, "Free preview".
- **Program.** Every lesson with its topics, their format icons and durations. Each academy
  styles it differently: a folio for `coffee`, a timeline for `oncall`, a mission map
  ("Your mission map") for `nightsky`, concentric orbits for `gravity`, an atlas table of contents for `poland`, a timeline for `ulam`. Lessons are called chapters, modules, missions or notebooks.
- **About the course.** The course description, rendered from Markdown.
- **Enrol card.** See below.

The site header and footer are the same as on the [landing page](/learners/home/).

## Progress for returning learners

If the learner has a session cookie, the page asks the API for the learner's program and
progress (and gives up after 0.9 seconds, rendering the public page instead). When the learner
has access to the course:

- the header shows the completion percentage;
- the primary button reads "Continue learning" and opens the first topic that is not completed;
- topics in the program show whether they are done.

A learner without a session sees the public page; "Start learning" opens `/learn/<id>`, which
signs the visitor in (as the demo student on demo tenants) and redirects to the right topic. See
[course player](/learners/course-player/).

## What changed since you were here

For a returning learner of a course that is kept in sync with its sources, a **What changed in this
course** list appears above the program. Every entry says its status in words and links to the
lesson where it still exists:

- **Updated since you completed it, 9 October 2026** (a lesson changed after you completed it);
- **New since you finished** (a lesson was added after you finished the course; your completion
  stays);
- **Retired lesson, no longer part of the course** (a lesson you had completed was removed; it stays
  in your history and is not linked);
- **The source of this lesson changed on …; an update is under review** (only when the author turned
  the marker on).

The list is hidden when there is nothing to show, and it never changes your progress. The lesson
page has the matching notice and the "Mark as reviewed" button, see
[course player](/learners/course-player/).

## Enrol card

The card titled "Demo access" lists the number of lessons and topics, the formats used, "Progress
saved as you go" and "Every lesson open in this demo". It shows the course price, or the first
shop product's price, and an "Open the course" button that goes to the player.

<Aside type="caution" title="No purchase in the reference frontend">
The reference frontend has no cart or checkout. The card says so: "Payments run through the shop
service in production; this demo opens the course directly." Access is granted by the API, not
by the frontend: on demo tenants the demo student gets access to every published course; on
other tenants an administrator grants access, or the learner buys through the
[legacy React app](/learners/legacy-react-app/), which still has the cart. The planned
Sylius-based commerce is on the [roadmap](/roadmap/).
</Aside>

A signed-in learner without access to the course can still open its free-preview topics; see
[preview mode](/learners/course-player/#preview-mode).
