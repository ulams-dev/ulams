When a part of a course has been hard for you, for example you answered a question wrongly more than once, the lesson can show an **Extra help for you** card above the lesson. It holds a short explanation of the idea in another way, and sometimes a worked example, a practice question or a question to answer in your own words. Everything in it is written by AI from the course's own sources and says so.

<Aside>
The card never changes your results, progress or certificates. You can ignore it, hide it or switch it off for good in [Personalisation and privacy](/learners/personalisation/).
</Aside>

## What is on the card

- **A label** such as "AI-generated, based on this course's sources" next to the title.
- **The help itself**: a short summary, then blocks with their own headings. Under each block, **Sources** names the sections of the course it is based on. The names are plain text.
- **Why am I seeing this?** A collapsed list of the reasons in plain language, for example "You answered this question wrongly 2 times".
- **This helped** and **Not helpful**: your rating, kept for the next visit. It helps the author see what works.
- **Hide extra help for this lesson**: asks to confirm, then removes the card. It does not come back for this lesson.

If a lesson has more than one card, each is numbered, for example "Extra help for you (1 of 2)". A card that is still being written says "Preparing an explanation…" and appears on your next visit.

## The practice question

Pick one answer and choose **Check**. The result is written in words ("Correct." or "Not quite.") together with the explanation, and the option you chose is marked with the same word, not only a colour. You can change your answer and check again. What you choose is not recorded or sent anywhere.

The question for your own words has a notes box that stays on your device; nothing is sent. Only the box described under [Explain it in your own words](#explain-it-in-your-own-words) sends your text, and only when you choose **Get feedback**.

## Explain it in your own words

At the end of a ready card, **Explain it in your own words** lets you write what you understood and get feedback on it.

1. Write at least 10 characters in the box (up to 2 000). The counter under it shows how many you have used.
2. Read the sentence above the button: your text is sent to the AI provider to give feedback, so do not include personal details.
3. Choose **Get feedback**. You get **What you got right**, **What is missing**, a question to think about next and the **Sources** of the course the feedback is based on. It is labelled as AI feedback.

Your text is sent once and is not stored: not by the academy, not in your browser. It is cleared from the box as soon as the feedback arrives; choose **Explain it again** to write another try. You can ask for feedback a limited number of times a day. When the limit is reached, or when AI feedback is switched off, the page says so in words and keeps what you wrote so you can try again later.

## Lessons made with AI assistance

A lesson that the author built with the AI Course Builder says "Created with AI assistance and reviewed by the author" at the end of the lesson. An academy can switch this label off in its settings.

## With a keyboard or a screen reader

The card is a labelled region with a heading. Every control is a native button, radio button or disclosure that you reach with Tab. Space or Enter presses it, and the rating buttons say whether they are pressed. After you hide the card, a short message is read out and focus moves to the lesson heading. The practice answers can be changed with the arrow keys. Nothing on the card moves when your system asks for reduced motion.

## Language

The card is shown in the language of the course (English, Polish or Chinese); the reasons come from the academy in the same language.
