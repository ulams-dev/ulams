The learner site is built to WCAG 2.2 level AA. This page lists what you can use and what to do when something gets in your way. The academy's own accessibility statement is at `/accessibility`, linked in the footer of every page.

## Keyboard

Everything works without a mouse.

| Key | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Tab, Shift+Tab | everywhere | Move to the next or previous control. The first stop on every page is **Skip to content** |
| Enter or Space | buttons, disclosures | Press the button, open or close the section |
| Arrow keys | answer options, lesson view choices | Move between the options of one group |
| Space or Enter | **This helped**, **Not helpful** | Switch the choice on or off; the state is read out |
| Ctrl+Enter | tutor question field | Send the question |
| Escape | tutor panel, lesson view | Close it and return to the button that opened it |

Focus is always visible, and a sticky bar never covers the control you are on.

## Lesson view

The **Lesson view** button at the top of a lesson sets text spacing, shows a reading in parts, switches to step by step navigation and opens hints at the start of an exercise. See [Lesson view](/learners/lesson-view/). The choices are yours and change no results.

## Reduced motion

When your system asks for reduced motion (macOS **Reduce motion**, Windows **Show animations**, Android **Remove animations**), the site stops animating: cards appear in place, lesson parts change without a transition, and the tutor panel opens without sliding. Nothing moves on its own.

## Zoom, text size and contrast

Pages reflow to a single column at 320 CSS pixels and at 200 % zoom without sideways scrolling. Status is written in words ("Overdue by 3 days"), never by colour alone. Light and dark themes meet the AA contrast ratios.

## Language

The interface is available in English, Polish and Chinese. Account pages follow the academy's default language; a lesson follows the language of its course.

## AI-generated help

Extra help and tutor answers are labelled as AI-generated, say why you see them and cite the lesson they come from. The labels and the **Why am I seeing this?** disclosure are plain text and work with a screen reader.

## Report a barrier

If something is hard or impossible to use, open `/accessibility` on your academy's site and write to the address listed there. Say which page you were on, what you wanted to do, what stopped you, and which browser and assistive technology you use. If the academy has not listed an address, use its contact form and mention accessibility. If you are not satisfied with the reply, the statement names who you can complain to.
