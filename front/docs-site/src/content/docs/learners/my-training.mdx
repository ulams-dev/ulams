The account page links to three pages that show what the academy expects of you and what you have
achieved. The summary on `/account` says, for example, "2 trainings due this month", "3 certificates"
and "5 skills", and links to each page.

## My training (`/account/training`)

- **Required training** lists the courses you were asked to complete. The status is written out:
  "Assigned", "Due soon" (within 14 days), "Overdue by 3 days", "Completed" or "Cancelled", with the due
  date. Overdue training comes first. Open training has a **Start** or **Continue** link; completed
  training links to the certificate that satisfied it.
- **Learning paths** show the steps of each path as Done, Available or Locked. A locked step says what
  unlocks it ("Unlocks after: Tools").
- **Certificates** (`#certificates`) lists each certificate with when it was issued, when it is valid
  until (or "Does not expire"), the course version and its status: "Valid", "Expiring in 12 days",
  "Expired" or "Revoked on 1 September 2026". **Download PDF** saves the certificate, **Verify** opens
  the public verification page, and **Copy badge code** copies a snippet for your own page (see
  [the certificate badge](/extending/web-components/#the-certificate-badge)). A revoked certificate has
  no badge code.

Every block says what went wrong when it cannot be loaded and offers "Try again"; the others still show.

## My skills (`/account/skills`)

Skills confirmed by the courses you completed, grouped by area, with the code, when you earned the
skill, when it lapses (it lapses with the certificate that proves it, or "Does not lapse") and the
courses that prove it. With no skills yet the page links to the catalogue.

## My team (`/account/team`)

Only managers see this page; for everyone else it is a 404 and there is no link. It is read-only: the
required training of your direct reports with the learner, course, status and due date, overdue first,
and a filter by status. Assigning training stays in the admin panel.

## The certificate PDF

`/account/certificates/{id}.pdf` downloads the PDF of one of your own certificates. Anyone else's id
gives a 404. The file is not cached.

<Aside>
The interface follows the tenant's default language (English, Polish or Chinese). The finish page of a
course uses the language of the course.
</Aside>
