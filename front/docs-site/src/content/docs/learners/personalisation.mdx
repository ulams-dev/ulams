An academy can follow how you move through a course to notice when a part is hard for you or when you have been away for a while. It then offers short extra help or sends one reminder. `/account/personalisation` shows everything this means for you and lets you change it. You reach it from [My learning](/learners/account/). It needs a sign-in.

<Aside>
This never changes your results, certificates or access to a course. It only decides whether you are offered help or a reminder.
</Aside>

## Where you are

For each course where something is flagged, the page says it in words, not colours: "Needs a closer look" when some parts have been hard, "Taking a break" when you have been away for a while. Under it, **Why you are seeing this** lists the reasons in plain language, for example "2 failed attempts on question 3 (Rebasing onto main)". If nothing is flagged, the page says so.

## Your choices

| Choice | What it does |
|---|---|
| Follow my learning | Off: nothing more is recorded about how you move through courses. Tick "Also delete what was recorded so far" to remove earlier records when you switch it off |
| Offer me extra help | Short explanations and practice built from the course's own sources when a part is hard. Always labelled as AI-generated, with the sources and the reason |
| Send me a reminder when I have been away | At most one per course a week, in the app and by e-mail |
| I agree to this | Only shown when the academy records nothing until you agree. You can withdraw at any time |

Choose **Save my choices**. They apply from then on.

## Your data

The page tells you how long activity (180 days by default) and history (365 days) are kept before they are deleted automatically. **Download my data (JSON)** gives you a file with everything stored about your learning in this academy. **Delete my data** removes it all after you tick the confirmation box; your choices above stay as they were. Deleting your account deletes this data as well.
