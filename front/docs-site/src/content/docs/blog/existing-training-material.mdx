Nobody starts from scratch. Every organisation already has a cupboard full of old training packages 😄

ULAMS handles them:

- SCORM 1.2 and 2004
- H5P
- cmi5

and lets you mix them with the rest of a course: a text lesson, an interactive activity, an existing package — all in one place.

In the video: the H5P branching scenario "Who does what?" opened as a learner and marked complete. No AI, just the platform doing its job — this part also comes from Wellms and has worked for years.

On a rollout I still start with the client's own material: does it open, does it store a score, does it report completion correctly. A spec is one thing, real packages are another.

What's in your libraries? SCORM, H5P, video, PDF, or your own apps?
