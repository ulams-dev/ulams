From runbook to course in 5 steps — no editing tricks (well, almost: I sped up generation and labelled it 😄).

- upload the document
- set the brief: audience, level, minutes
- review the proposed structure
- read the lessons and questions
- open the preview before publishing anything

What's real: a real model, a real document, the real Studio. Cost of the whole proposal: 4 cents. Every lesson points to the passage it came from.

The important part stays with a human: what the learner should learn and whether the text explains it well. AI drafts. I decide.
