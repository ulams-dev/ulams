🎯 Looking for 3 teams to talk about ULAMS.

Over 10 days I showed:

- a course from documentation
- a source behind every sentence
- working without AI
- the API and your own frontend
- starting it with one command
- an update when the docs change

Building is what I enjoy, but I want to build for real problems, not imagined ones. I'm most curious about SaaS companies, developer tools and teams that teach customers technical things.

I'd love to hear:

- what you build training from today,
- who creates it and who keeps it up to date,
- which integrations you need,
- what annoys you most in your current tool.

If this is your topic, send me a DM or leave a comment. A short description of your use case is enough.

Demo and contact: [https://ulams.app/](https://ulams.app/)

Thanks for following along for these 10 days 🚀
