**My profile** is your own account page. It is not in the left menu: open it from the avatar
menu in the top-right corner. The screen needs the `user_read_self` permission and has three
tabs, each with its own URL (`/my-profile/:tab`; `/my-profile` opens `general`).

<Screenshot name="admin-my-profile-tab" alt="The My profile screen with General, Change password and User settings tabs" />

## General (`/my-profile/general`)

- **First name** and **Last name**.
- Extra fields defined in [User fields](/admin/users/#user-fields).
- **Avatar**: upload an image (`POST /api/profile/upload-avatar`), pick one from the
  [file library](/admin/files/) (folder `avatars/{your id}`) or delete it.

**Submit** saves the form with `PUT /api/profile/me`. Your email and roles cannot be changed
here; another administrator changes them on [Users](/admin/users/).

## Change password (`/my-profile/change_password`)

Enter your **current password**, the **new password** and its **confirmation**. The API
(`PUT /api/profile/password`) checks that the current password matches and that the new one
has at least 6 characters, and dispatches the `PasswordChanged` event (which has an email
template; see [Templates](/admin/templates/)).

If you have forgotten your password, sign out and use **Forgot password** on the sign-in
screen ([The admin panel](/admin/#forgotten-password)).

## User settings (`/my-profile/user_settings`)

A key–value table of settings stored on your account (`/api/admin/users/{id}/settings`).
Frontends use these to remember per-user preferences. The toolbar has two actions:

- **New** adds or changes the given keys and keeps the others (`PATCH`).
- **Overwrite** replaces all your settings with the keys you enter (`PUT`).

The same settings exist for every user, but the panel lets you edit only your own; the
corresponding tab on other users' edit screens is disabled in the code.
