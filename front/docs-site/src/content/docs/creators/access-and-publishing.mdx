Two questions decide what a learner can do with a course: **can they see it** (the catalogue and the
course page) and **can they open it** (the lessons). Status and visibility answer the first; access
answers the second.

## Course status

Set the status on the course's **Attributes** tab.

| Status | Effect |
|---|---|
| Draft | Only in the admin panel. Learners cannot see or open it. |
| Published | Visible in the catalogue; people with access can take it. Publishing sends the "course published" event. |
| `published_unactivated` | Counts as published, and a daily job switches it to Published once **Active from** has passed (and **Active to** has not). In the English admin it is currently labelled "Opublikowany nieaktywny". |
| Archived | Hidden from learners; set it back to Published to reopen the course. |

Whether a published course shows up for people who are not signed in depends on the platform's
visibility setting (`platform_visibility`, set by an administrator). With `public` (the default) new
courses are listed for everyone; with `registered`, new courses are only visible to people who have
access to them.

### Public courses

When an administrator enables the **Is public** switch for course forms, you can mark a course as
public on the **Attributes** tab. A course that is both public and published can be opened by anyone,
without an account and without being granted access.

### Free preview topics

Topics marked **Able to preview** open for everyone who can see the course, so learners can try it
before they get access. See [Lessons and topics](/creators/lessons-and-topics/#fields-every-topic-has).

## Give access to users and groups

<Steps>

1. Open the course editor's **Access** tab (`/courses/list/<id>/access`).
2. Add people under **Users** and whole groups under **Groups**.
3. Click **Submit**. The lists you submit replace the previous ones.

</Steps>

Removing a user or a group takes their access away. Adding and removing access sends the course
assigned/unassigned notifications, which an administrator can turn into e-mails in
[Notifications](/admin/notifications/).

## Access requests

Learners can ask for access to a course and add a message. The requests appear in
**Courses → Access Requests** (`/courses/access`) with the course, the learner, the date, the status
(pending or approved) and the extra data the learner sent.

- **Approve** grants the learner access to the course.
- **Delete** removes the request.

People with the approval permission are notified about new requests. An administrator can switch on
automatic approval in the settings (`auto_accept_access_request`), which grants access as soon as a
request arrives; use it for free courses that only require a sign-up.

<Screenshot name="admin-courses-access" alt="Access Requests with pending and approved requests" />

The request button is part of the legacy learner app; the reference front does not offer it yet.

## Invite people without an account

The **Users Attached without Account** tab (`/courses/list/<id>/user_submission`) takes e-mail
addresses of people who do not have an account yet. Each person receives an e-mail with instructions;
once they create an account with that address, they get access to the course.

The invitation e-mail uses a notification template that an administrator has to set up; see
[Notifications](/admin/notifications/).

## Prices and products

To sell a course, open its **Product** tab (shown with the sales permission). It is the same product
form as **Sales → Products**: name, price and tax, whether the product can be purchased, limits per
user and in total, related products and the product's media, categories and tags. Learners who buy
the product get access to the course once the payment is confirmed.

<Aside>
The product and payment screens are described in the [administrator guide](/admin/). Commerce is planned
to move to a separate service later ([roadmap](/roadmap/)); the LMS keeps deciding who has access.
</Aside>
