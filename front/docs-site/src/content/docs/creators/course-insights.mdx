The **Insights** page shows how learners are doing in one of your courses: where they progress, where they get
stuck and whether the extra help worked. It works for **any course you author**, whether or not you built it with
the Course Builder, and every figure is an aggregate. You see people by name only if an administrator gave you the
permission (see [Individual learners](#individual-learners)).

## Before you start

- An administrator has to switch **Learner Insights** on for the academy
  ([settings](/admin/learner-insights/)). It is off by default and records nothing while it is off. When it is off
  the page says so and shows nothing.
- You need to be an **author of the course**, or hold the Learner Insights permission. Anyone else gets a message
  that explains this.
- Figures start to appear once learners have used the course. A new course shows an empty state.

## Open the page

- In the studio, open the course and choose **Insights** in the left rail. From the Course Builder this is the
  **Insights** item of a session that has a course.
- In the admin, open the course and choose the **Insights** tab. It shows a short summary (completion, who needs
  support and the five sections that confuse people most) and **Open full insights** takes you to this page.
- The address is `/studio/courses/<course id>/insights`.

Use **Period** to switch between the last 7, 30 and 90 days. The page reads the same in English, Polish and
Simplified Chinese: it follows your browser language, or add `?lang=pl` or `?lang=zh` to the address, or use the
language links at the top.

## What you see

| Section | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Progress and completion | How many learners are enrolled, how many were active in the period, how many finished and the completion rate. |
| Quiz performance | Finished attempts, the pass rate and the average score of all quizzes of the course together. |
| Who needs support | How many learners are on track, struggling or at risk of dropping out. |
| Sections that confuse people | Every section ranked by the share of its active learners who struggled, with the share who got extra help, the share who recovered afterwards and the main reasons. |
| Personal help and nudges | How much extra help was shown and rated, how many reminders were sent and opened, and how many learners recovered afterwards. |
| Learners who are struggling or at risk | Individual data, only for people who are allowed to see it. |

Every rate is written as a number, for example "41%". The small bar next to it is only a picture of the same number,
so nothing depends on colour. A rate that says **Not enough data** has too few cases behind it to be meaningful.

## The reasons

A section's reasons say what kind of difficulty learners had there. They come from fixed rules, never from
anything a learner typed:

- **Repeated failed attempts**: several wrong answers on a quiz question.
- **Takes much longer than most**: a lesson that takes learners far longer than the median.
- **Left open while working elsewhere**: a lesson learners open and then drop.
- **Replays or revisits**: learners go back to a video or lesson several times.
- **Inactive for days** and **Several hard parts** describe a learner rather than a section.

Use them to decide what to improve: a question with repeated failures may need a clearer stem, a slow lesson may be
too long.

## Privacy: small groups are hidden

Groups smaller than **10 learners** are hidden. This is decided by the server, not by the page: the figures are never
sent, so there is nothing to find in the page source. In practice:

- A course with fewer than 10 enrolled learners shows only an explanation.
- A section with fewer than 10 active learners in the period shows **Hidden: fewer than 10 learners** and no figures.
- A recovery rate appears once at least 10 cases are closed.

An administrator can change the minimum (`LEARNER_INSIGHTS_MIN_GROUP_SIZE`). The page always states the number in
use.

## Individual learners

The list of learners who are struggling or at risk, with the reasons and since when, is shown only to:

- people with the permission `learner_insights_view_individuals` (administrators by default), and
- authors of the course, when the academy turned on `learner_insights.instructors_see_individuals` (off by default).

Everyone else sees a sentence explaining why the list is hidden; the aggregates stay. **Show activity** next to a
learner opens that learner's recorded activity (what they opened, answered and finished, newest first). That view is
for administrators only. Use this to reach out and help, not to rank people. Learners can switch recording off
in their own settings, and then nothing about them appears here.

## Good to know

- Insights show what happened, not why a learner did something. A struggling learner is not a failing learner, and
  a status never changes grades, access or certificates.
- Nothing on this page, and none of the data behind it, is ever sent to an AI provider.
- The page is built for keyboards and screen readers: tables have captions and headers, wide tables scroll
  sideways and take the keyboard focus, and changes are announced.
