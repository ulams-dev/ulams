The Course Builder asks one fixed question about price: **free or paid**. It never sells anything on
its own: a product is created only for a paid course with a price you confirmed, and it stays
hidden until you publish.

## Choose free or paid

- **Free** (the default): learners enrol without paying. No product is created.
- **Paid**: type the price in the currency of your shop (set in the payments settings). If you leave
  it empty, the assistant suggests a starting price once your course is drafted, with a short reason.
  A suggestion is only a suggestion: it is marked **suggested** and it is not used until you press
  **Use this price** or type your own in the **Course brief** panel.

You can change the price at any time with **Edit** on the **Price** row of the brief. Changing the price
does not make the outline or the lessons out of date.

## What happens when you apply and publish

1. **Apply to my academy** creates the course unpublished. For a paid course with a confirmed price it also
   creates a **product** for the course in **Sales → Products**, not yet purchasable.
2. If the shop cannot sell the price (for example a currency other than the shop's), the course is still
   applied and the apply notes tell you the product was not created.
3. **Publish course** publishes the course and makes its product purchasable. Learners who buy it get access
   after the payment is confirmed, as for any product.

<Aside type="note">
Switching a course back to free and applying again hides the product. Existing buyers keep their access.
</Aside>

## For developers

The builder talks to commerce through the `CommerceProvider` interface of the `commerce` package; the
built-in cart is the default adapter and a Sylius adapter is planned. See the
[commerce package](/developers/api-packages/#commerce).
