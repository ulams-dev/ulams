01

The Mathematical Core of Error Budgets

Alerting on raw CPU, memory, or simple error rate spikes leads to alert fatigue. High-performing engineering teams alert on Error Budget Burn Rate. Before engineering the alert rules, we anchor our foundational mathematical relationships:

SLI (Indicator)

A quantifiable metric of service performance (e.g. ratio of successful HTTP requests to total).

SLO (Objective)

Target reliability percentage committed to over a sliding evaluation window (e.g., 99.9% over 30d).

Error Budget

Allowable unreliability: 100% - SLO. The currency spent during changes and incidents.

FORMULATION: ALLOWED DOWNTIME CALCULATION SLO = 99.9% (30-DAY WINDOW)
Error_Budget_% = 100% - SLO = 100% - 99.9% = 0.1% Allowed_Downtime = (1 - 0.999) × 30 days × 24h × 60m Allowed_Downtime = 0.001 × 43,200 min = 43.2 minutes

02

Why Single-Window Alerting Fails

If you evaluate burn rate using only a single window, you hit the fundamental trade-off of alerting theory: Detection Latency versus Reset Latency.

TELEMETRY WINDOW FAILURE MODES
warning FLAW 1: SHORT WINDOW ONLY (e.g., 5 min)

Alerts fire instantaneously during temporary blips. Drops immediately when transient error drops. Causes extreme alert fatigue without protecting long-term budget health.

schedule FLAW 2: LONG WINDOW ONLY (e.g., 1 hour)

Takes too long to alert during catastrophic complete outages. Worse: after the incident is fixed, the alert continues paging on-call engineers for 50+ minutes until the trailing average falls.

03

The Multi-Window Multi-Burn-Rate Solution

Google SRE solves this by requiring both a Long Window AND a Short Window to breach threshold simultaneously. The long window ensures significant budget was actually consumed; the short window ensures the incident is still happening right now.

SEVERITY BURN RATE BUDGET CONSUMED LONG WINDOW SHORT WINDOW (1/12th) ACTION
CRITICAL 14.4x 2% in 1 hour 1 hour 5 minutes Page Commander
HIGH 6.0x 5% in 6 hours 6 hours 30 minutes File High Ticket
WARNING 1.0x 10% in 3 days 3 days (36h) 2 hours Slack Digest
code alert_rules.rules.yaml (PromQL Production Formulation) 
# Critical SEV-1: 14.4x Burn Rate Alert (1h Long Window, 5m Short Window)
- alert: PaymentGatewayHighErrorBudgetBurn
  expr: (
    # Long Window Condition (1h)
    job:slo_errors_per_request:ratio_rate1h{job="payment-gw"} > (14.4 * (1 - 0.999))
    and
    # Short Window Condition (5m) ensures issue is still active right now!
    job:slo_errors_per_request:ratio_rate5m{job="payment-gw"} > (14.4 * (1 - 0.999))
  )
  for: 2m
  labels:
    severity: page
    role: incident_commander
  annotations:
    summary: "High error budget burn rate (14.4x over 1h & 5m)"
    runbook_url: "https://oncall.academy/runbooks/payments-burn-rate"
