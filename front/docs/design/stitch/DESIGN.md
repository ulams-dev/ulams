## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the sensory, scholarly, and contemplative essence of independent print periodicals (such as *Cereal*, *Drift*, and *Apartamento*). It fuses tactile editorial traditions with digital learning architecture. The system serves baristas, roasters, agronomists, and discerning enthusiasts seeking mastery over coffee culture and craft.

The visual style is **Tactile Editorial Minimalism**:
- **Tangible Paper Metaphor:** Surfaces mimic unbleached archival paper, linen bookbindings, and letterpress impressions rather than digital screen layers.
- **Academic Serenity:** Broad margins, deliberate pauses, and disciplined typographic hierarchy cultivate deep focus and slow study.
- **Artisanal Restraint:** Decorative elements are restricted to physical bookbinding artifacts: hairline separator rules, deckle-edge dividers, Roman numeral indexes, and small-caps chapter headers.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from raw terroir, ceramic brewing vessels, and weathered print artifacts:

- **Neutral (#2B1D14 - Deep Roast Espresso):** The master text and structural ink color. A concentrated, soot-tinged roast brown replacing standard digital black.
- **Canvas / Background (#F6F1E9 - Heavy Unbleached Cotton):** The default screen canvas. A tactile, creamy off-white reminiscent of heavy archival stock.
- **Tertiary (#EDE5D8 - Pressed Parchment):** Used for inset panels, highlighted text excerpts, interactive callouts, and progress cards.
- **Primary Accent (#C2552D - Fired Terracotta):** Represents heat, clay drippers, and ripe coffee cherries. Applied to key interactive triggers, drop caps, active reading progress indicators, and primary course actions.
- **Secondary Accent (#7A8B6F - Washed Sage):** Derived from unroasted green coffee beans and shade trees. Used for completed states, sensory tasting notes, audio indicator pulses, and botanical metadata.
- **Border / Hairline Ink (#D9CFC2 - Archival Rule):** Ultra-subtle divider color engineered for 1px hairline rules, framing boxes, and magazine layout grids without digital harshness.

## Typography

The typographic system contrasts the romantic gravitas of high-contrast editorial serifs with the crisp utility of a humanist sans serif:

- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display):** Evokes mid-century European gastronomic journals. Used for volume designations, monograph titles, quotes, and chapter intros. Headline italics (`font-style: italic`) are used deliberately for French/Italian coffee vocabulary, botanical species classifications (*Coffea arabica*), and sensory impressions.
- **Interface & Reading Body (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for warm geometric clarity and open counters. Preserves effortless legibility across step-by-step extraction guides, brewing formulas, and long-form learning transcripts.
- **Typographic Ornaments:**
  - **Drop Caps:** When starting long-form lesson sections, the initial letter renders in 64px `Playfair Display` Terracotta (`#C2552D`), floated left with a 3-line indent.
  - **Chapter Headers:** Formatted via `label-chapter` with wide tracking and Roman numeral indicators (`CHAPTER IV — EXTRACTION DYNAMICS`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system borrows structural balance from asymmetric, multi-column print editorial grids:

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop relies on an asymmetric 12-column layout. Editorial content commonly follows an offset 8/4 or 7/5 split: 7 or 8 columns dedicated to immersive reading or video feed, and 4 or 5 columns reserved for annotations, tasting wheels, botanical marginalia, or study prompts.
- **Rhythm & White Space:** Spacing is generous. Content sections are delineated by whitespace rather than heavy background boxes. Vertical margins between syllabus chapters expand to `space-xl` (48px) to provide natural intellectual breaks.
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 56px (`margin`) outer borders, 32px (`gutter`) column spacing.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 32px margins, 24px gutters. Asymmetric sidebars flow into collapsable bottom drawers.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column vertical folio, 20px (`margin-mobile`) side gutters. Content maintains continuous unhurried reading margins.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic drop shadows, neon glow halos, and glossy skeuomorphism. Depth is achieved strictly through **Tonal Layering** and **Archival Debossing**:

- **Layer Stacking (Flat Tactility):** 
  - Base layer: Warm off-white canvas (`#F6F1E9`).
  - Elevated surfaces (recipe cards, audio players, course cards): Parchment containers (`#EDE5D8`) paired with a crisp 1px hairline border (`#D9CFC2`).
- **Debossed Indentations:** Active input fields and progress tracks use an inset hairline border (`1px solid #D9CFC2`) with a background tint shifted 2% darker than the parent container, evoking an intaglio paper press mark.
- **Hover Transitions:** Interactive cards lift subtly via a micro-translation (`translateY(-2px)`) accompanied by an ultra-soft, warm umber ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(43, 29, 20, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The shape system prioritizes crisp, bookbound architecture over hyper-rounded consumer digital trends:

- **Soft Contours (`roundedness: 1`):** Core interactive components (buttons, badges, inputs, lesson tiles) feature a restrained 0.25rem (4px) corner radius, recalling die-cut paper labels, book plates, and index cards.
- **Photographic Frames:** Course photography and video frames utilize strict 0px or 4px radii, framed with delicate 1px inner hairline borders to evoke museum-mounted prints.
- **Media Badge Tags:** Pill shapes are avoided in favor of architectural rectangular stamps with 2px corner softening (`rounded-sm`), mirroring vintage postage stamps or library catalogue card markers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Field Stamp):** Background in Terracotta (`#C2552D`), text in Canvas (`#F6F1E9`). Rectangular with 4px border radius. Hover shifts to deeper roast-brick (`#A84521`).
- **Secondary / Ghost (Bookbinder's Outline):** Transparent background, 1px hairline border in `#2B1D14`, text in `#2B1D14`. On hover, fills with Parchment (`#EDE5D8`).
- **Text Link:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` medium in Terracotta (`#C2552D`) with a continuous hairline underline shifted 4px below text.

### Media & Modality Badges
- **Format Indicators:** Tags for media types (Video, Audio, Interactive, Reading, SCORM, Project). 
- **Styling:** Set in `label-caps` (11px uppercase), 4px padding-y, 8px padding-x, 2px radius. Background in Parchment (`#EDE5D8`), 1px border in Archival Rule (`#D9CFC2`), text in Deep Roast (`#2B1D14`). Audio formats feature a small Sage dot (`#7A8B6F`).

### Table of Contents (Magazine Syllabus)
- Delineated by 1px horizontal hairline rules (`#D9CFC2`).
- Left column displays Roman numerals (`I`, `II`, `III`) in Playfair Display Terracotta. Center column hosts the lesson title in Playfair Display (20px) with lesson duration and topic description in Plus Jakarta Sans. Right column features completion stamps or media badge tags.

### Pull Quotes & Marginalia
- **Pull Quotes:** Centered or margin-aligned, rendered in `editorial-quote` Playfair Display italic. Top and bottom bounded by 40px centered hairline rules (`#D9CFC2`). 
- **Marginalia / Captions:** Set in `caption-editorial` italic with a 1px vertical Sage rule to the left, referencing harvest altitudes, roast dates, or French/Italian terminology.

### Cards & Frames
- **Course & Lesson Cards:** Framed in 1px `#D9CFC2` on `#EDE5D8` parchment surface. Photography sits inside a 6px inner white inset margin, resembling tipped-in photographic plates in antique art books.

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Fields:** Subtle unbleached surface (`#FDFCFA`), 1px hairline border (`#D9CFC2`), focus ring rendered as a precise double-line offset or 1px Terracotta border without fuzzy focus glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Hand-pressed appearance with 1px Deep Roast borders. Checked state reveals a solid Terracotta square/disc inset by 3px.
