The 1:16 Golden Ratio

Target read time: 6 mins · SCAA Standards · Refined today by AI Architect

In specialty coffee brewing, the water-to-coffee ratio is the foundational dial controlling both total dissolution and thermodynamic efficiency. While modern extraction models accommodate variances from 1:14 up to 1:18, the benchmark standard accepted across empirical cupping is the classic 1:16 proportion.

By weight, every gram of roasted and ground dry mass demands sixteen grams of high-purity water heated to roughly 93.5°C. When calculated accurately, this delivers total dissolved solids (TDS) within the idealized 1.15% to 1.35% range without tipping into bitter polyphenolic astringency. link §3.1 Ratios
