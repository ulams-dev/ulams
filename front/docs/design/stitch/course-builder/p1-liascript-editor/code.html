The 1:16 ratio

In specialty coffee brewing, the water-to-coffee ratio is the foundational dial controlling dissolution kinetics. By modulating mass interactions within the brew chamber, the barista regulates total soluble yield, beverage clarity, and aromatic balance before brew temperature or agitation comes into play.
