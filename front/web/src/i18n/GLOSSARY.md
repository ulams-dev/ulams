stay in English everywhere: ulams, Claude, Claude Code, MCP, H5P, SCORM, cmi5, xAPI, LTI, OpenAPI, Docker, Git,
GitHub, REST API, SDK, CLI.

Style: Polish addresses the reader as "Ty" (capitalised) in copy and uses the second person singular imperative on
buttons. Chinese uses 你 (not 您), full-width punctuation in running text and a space between Chinese and Latin
words or numbers. Numbers keep the local decimal mark in prose (PL: 99,95 %; ZH: 99.95 %).
